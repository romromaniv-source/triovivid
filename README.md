# Privacy Policy for Triovivid

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Triovivid** ("we," "our," or "the app") handles your information. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Data Collection
**Triovivid does not collect, store, or share any personal data.** 

* We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.
* We do not collect usage analytics, crash logs, device identifiers, or location data.
* Any progress, settings, or preferences you create within the app are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to external servers.

## 2. Third-Party Services
Triovivid does not integrate with third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers. No information is shared with third parties.

## 3. Children’s Privacy
Because Triovivid does not collect any personal information from anyone, the app is safe for users of all ages and complies with children's privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
